## Evidence status rules

- **Pending:** a decision is needed but no blocking scientific artifact is necessarily absent.
- **Blocked:** completion depends on missing research data, original artifacts, or external approval.
- **Accepted:** use only after the named stakeholder, date, scope, decision, and supporting artifact are recorded.
- **Rejected/revise:** retain the reason and update the requirement; do not silently replace it.
